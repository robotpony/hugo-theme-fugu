A patty is a formula, not a fixed recipe.

```formula
can          | Beans   | 1 can         | black, pinto, chickpea
+ bowl       | Starch  | 1 cup         | rice, mash, quinoa
+ carrot     | Veg     | 1 cup         | carrot, peas, corn
+ crumbs egg | Binder  | ½ cup + 1 egg | or gluten + oats
= patty      | Patties | 6
```

## Mechanic

**Per can of beans, 1 cup cooked starch, 1 cup vegetables, ½ cup breadcrumbs, and 1 egg.** The beans are the body, the starch makes the mix tacky, and the binder holds it together. [[Kewpie mayonnaise]] is good on top.

## Patties

- 1 can (540 ml / 19 oz) black beans, drained (~250 g)
- 160 g (1 cup) cooked [[Plain rice]]
- 1 medium carrot, grated (~60 g, ½ cup)
- 1 egg, beaten
- 50 g (½ cup) dry breadcrumbs
- 1 tbsp neutral oil, for frying

Mash the beans, leaving a quarter in pieces. Stir in the rice and carrot, then the egg and breadcrumbs. Shape into 6 patties about 2 cm (¾ in) thick and chill for 30 minutes. Fry over medium, 4–5 minutes per side, at about 180°C (350°F).

## Quick sauce

- 60 ml (¼ cup) mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp ketchup
- 1 tsp pickle brine

Stir together.

## To serve

In buns with the sauce and pickles.

## Variations

**Potato instead of rice.** Use 1 cup cold mashed potato.

## Notes

- Water is what breaks a patty: dry every wet ingredient first.
