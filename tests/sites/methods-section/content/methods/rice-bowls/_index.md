A rice bowl is rice with something on it. Each part has its own method, and a build picks one of each.

| Year | Bowl | What it was |
|---|---|---|
| c. 1900 | [Donburi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donburi) | Rice under a simmered topping |
| 1950s | Bibimbap in restaurants | Rice with mixed toppings, no link |
| 1990 | Patty bowl | The [[Patty rice bowl]] build, which has its own era |
