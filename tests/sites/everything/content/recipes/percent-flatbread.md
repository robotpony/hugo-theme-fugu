A dough and a glaze, both written as percentages of their base.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | % | Per 600 g | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| AP flour | 100% | 600 g | Any flour works |
| Water | 70% | 420 g | |
| Salt | 2% | 12.5 g | |
| Yeast | 1.7% | 10 g | |
| Olive oil | 5.8% | 35 g | 25 g added with the liquid |

## Glaze

| Ingredient | % of shoyu | Example: 250 ml shoyu |
|---|---|---|
| Shoyu (soy sauce) | 100% | 250 ml |
| Mirin | 80–100% | 200–250 ml |
| Sugar | 40% | 100 g |

## Method

Rest 50 g of the dough. Bake at 200°C (400°F).
