Used by [[Plain rice]].

| Rice | Water per cup |
|---|---|
| Long-grain | 1½ cups |
| Short-grain | 1¼ cups |
| Brown | 2 cups |
| [[Plain rice\|Basic white]] | 1½ cups |

Short-grain against long-grain:

```flow
row: Short-grain
Rinse | three changes
Soak  | 30 min
Boil
Steam | 15 min
Rest  | 10 min
row: Long-grain
Rinse | once
Boil  | `1½ cups` water
Steam | 18 min
caption: Same pot, different patience
```
