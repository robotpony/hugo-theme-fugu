{{/* A blueprint's lead for its parts or builds: the body of lead.md in its parts/ or builds/ folder (content/methods/burgers/builds/lead.md), a headless page (`build: {render: never, list: never}`), so it has no page of its own and isn't a part or a build. Its folder can't have an _index.md instead, which would make it a section and take its parts or builds out of the blueprint (blueprint/of.html). [[Wiki links]] are rewritten to standard links first, as on the blueprint page. Takes a dict: blueprint (its section page), folder ("parts" or "builds"); returns the rendered HTML, or "" when there's no lead.md or it's empty. */}} {{ $out := "" }} {{ with site.GetPage (path.Join .blueprint.Path .folder "lead") }} {{ $md := .RawContent }} {{ $md = replaceRE `\[\[([^\]|]+)\|([^\]]+)\]\]` `[$2](<$1>)` $md }} {{ $md = replaceRE `\[\[([^\]]+)\]\]` `[$1](<$1>)` $md }} {{ with strings.TrimSpace $md }}{{ $out = $.blueprint.RenderString (dict "display" "block") . }}{{ end }} {{ end }} {{ return $out }}
