{{/* A blueprint's lead for its parts or builds: the body of the _index.md in its parts/ or builds/ folder (content/methods/burgers/builds/_index.md), a headless section (`build: {render: never, list: never}`), so it has no page of its own and isn't listed, and the folder's pages stay the blueprint's parts or builds (blueprint/of.html). [[Wiki links]] are rewritten to standard links first, as on the blueprint page.
