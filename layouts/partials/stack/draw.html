{{/* Draws a ```stack block as an exploded view: each layer from assets/icons/layers/.svg, top to bottom, pulled apart, the upper layers drawn over the lower. One layer per line, `key | label | role`, plus optional caption: and repeat: lines (`repeat: 3-5 x3`, a "× 3" bracket by layers 3 to 5). A key can be a pair, `bun-base + mayo`, drawn from bun-base+mayo.svg: a spread on its bun is its own drawing, not two layers on top of each other. hugo-warped's parse reads the block (the shared fence spec, fences/stack.md) and warns about anything it skips or doubts; this draws what it keeps, and an unknown key as a dashed placeholder. They're inlined, so currentColor and the theme tokens (--accent-soft, --surface) follow dark mode. Labels, leaders and the bracket carry plain defaults in currentColor; a site's CSS restyles them by class (.stack-label, .stack-role, .stack-lead, .stack-dot, .stack-bracket, .stack-times, .stack-cap).
