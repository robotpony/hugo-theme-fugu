This is Fugu's example site: a handful of recipes, an essay, reference pages, a Food Log month, and a burger blueprint, to show what the theme does with each.

The content comes from [Not a Chef](https://bruce-loves-to-cook.warpedvisions.org/), a family cookbook built on Fugu, and is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) (see `exampleSite/LICENSE`). Some pages were trimmed and links to pages not included here were removed.
