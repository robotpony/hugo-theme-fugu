The classic Canadian A&W Mozza burger has a sweet sauce.

## Mechanic

A **mayo base with roughly half as much red relish**, and a spoonful of Thousand Island to round it out. The relish brings the sweetness, sourness, and texture; the chili sauce in it gives the tomato tang that sets it apart from a plain relish mayo.

## Ingredients

- 125 ml (½ cup) mayonnaise
- 60–75 ml (4–5 tbsp) red relish: sweet pickle relish mixed with Heinz Chili Sauce
- 15–30 ml (1–2 tbsp) Thousand Island dressing
- A pinch of sugar (optional)

## Method

Stir everything together. Taste, and add the sugar if it isn't sweet enough.

## To serve

On both buns of a [[Mozza burger]].

## Notes

- [[Bruce's burger sauce]] was also developed for a mozza-style burger: sharper, with mustard, where this one is sweeter.
