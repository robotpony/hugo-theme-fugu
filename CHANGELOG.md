# Changelog

Notable changes to Fugu, newest first. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org); until `v1.0.0`, a minor version can change how a site renders or what it has to configure. Each release is a git tag (`v0.1.0`, …). PLAN.md §10 says when the first one lands.

## Unreleased

### Added

- A `cheese-american` stack layer: the `cheese` slice filled yellow (`--kit-yellow` edge, `--kit-yellow-top` face), for builds with a processed slice; `cheese` stays plain for mozzarella and cheddar.
- Stack layer pairs: a key `a + b` (spaces optional) draws `assets/icons/layers/a+b.svg`, for a spread drawn on its bun, which is its own drawing rather than two layers. A pair with no file warns and draws the placeholder, like an unknown key. New: `bun-top+mayo`, `bun-base+mayo`, `bun-top+sauce-burger`, `bun-base+sauce-burger`; the top bun is opened toward the viewer, its cut face showing the spread.
- Food colours in the stack kit: `ketchup`, `lettuce`, `lettuce-chunks`, `lettuce-shredded`, `mustard`, `pickle`, `sauce-ketchup-mustard` and `tomato` are filled soft green, red or yellow, and `mayo` and `sauce-burger` have new knife marks: a solid lip in `currentColor` and a faint echo line. `lettuce-shredded`'s strands are a solid fill inside a thinner line (0.6, was 0.9), so the green shows; `tomato`'s flesh and edge take the deeper red and its seed segments the paler one. They read `--kit-green`, `--kit-green-top`, `--kit-green-ink`, `--kit-red`, `--kit-red-top`, `--kit-yellow` and `--kit-yellow-top`, falling back to new `--fugu-kit-*` in `fugu.css` (light and dark), as the sear colours do.
- A `lettuce-chunks` stack layer: torn or chopped iceberg in a heap of wide pieces, with no pad under them, for builds that don't use a whole leaf. Its edge reaches its pieces (76.4, was 70), so leader dots clear them.
- A `ketchup` stack layer: the ketchup swirl from `sauce-ketchup-mustard` without the mustard, for builds that skip it.
- A blueprint's `parts/_index.md` and `builds/_index.md`, headless sections (`build: {render: never, list: never}`), lead its page's parts box (in place of `partsLead`) and its build cards (`div.bp-desc.bp-builds-lead`), with wiki links (`blueprint/lead.html`). Replaces `lead.md` in those folders, which is no longer read: rename it to `_index.md`. A headless section gets no breadcrumb (a new override of Blowfish's `partials/breadcrumbs.html`), and a page's feed link is its nearest section's that has one (`head.html`).
- `params.fugu.methods.partsLead`: a line at the top of a blueprint page's parts box (`.bp-desc` in `section.bp.bp-parts-only`), in Markdown, by default "Each part is its own method. A build picks one option from each." `""` leaves it out.
- A `lettuce-shredded` stack layer: a low pile of thin, curling, square-cut strands with a few strays at the base, for builds with shredded lettuce. Its edge reaches the strays (77.2), so leader dots clear them. The leaf `lettuce` layer's edge (`data-edge`) now reaches its frill (85.5, was 78.4), and `mayo` and `sauce-burger` reach their spread (64.7, was 60.2), so leader dots clear them.
- Recipes per part: a recipe's `part` (a name from its blueprint's `parts`, or a list, matched case-insensitively) lists it beside that part on the blueprint's page, with its time (`.part-recipes`, `.part-recipes-head`, `.part-recipe`, `.part-recipe-time`; `.part-recipes.is-empty` reads "No recipes for this part yet."). The full parts list is three columns (the recipes under the method below 820px). `blueprint/info.html` returns `byPart`, and its `loose` ("No build yet") now leaves out recipes with a part. The time is `total_time`, else `prep_time`, else `cook_time`, shortened as on a card (`partials/short-time.html`, which the card now uses too).
- The builds comparison has equal columns: a fixed layout, a `<colgroup>` with a set label column (`col.bc-label`), a "Part" corner (`.bc-corner`), wrapping row labels, and the heavier rule under the profile row running under its label too.
- The methods list sets each blueprint beside its builds (`.bp-row`, a stack of rows in `.bp-list`, one column under 820px). Builds are tiles in place of pills (`.bp-builds`, `.bp-builds-head` with a "Compare picks →" link to the comparison, `.build-tiles`, `.build-tile`, `.build-tile-art`, `.build-tile-name`, `.build-tile-profile`, `.build-origin.is-missing` reading "origin to come"), whole-card links with every stack drawn 58px wide on a shelf so heights compare; a sideways scroll-snap row under 820px. The blueprint block no longer lights up as a whole: its name (`.bp-name`, now with `.go`) and its part rows (`.part-row`, whole-row links; unwritten rows read "to come") answer hover instead. The blueprint page's "Builds compared" heading has `id="compare"`.

Every regular page in it is a method (`method/is.html`), reading like an essay (the reading sidebar, its siblings listed under the section's title, reading time on its card), and its page is the methods list (`layouts/methods/list.html`, through `method/list.html`), which the method chip and blueprint pages link to (`method/list-page.html`). The list's further reading comes from the methods tag. Methods in the essay section's `folder` still count. A "method" section role (`section/role.html`); About's numbers count methods; `frontmatter.py` and `drafts.py` know the section.
- Stack diagrams: a ```` ```stack ```` block (`layer | label | role` per line, top first, plus `caption:` and `repeat: 3-5 x3`) draws an exploded view of a build, with leader lines to labels and roles on the right (`render-codeblock-stack.html`, `partials/stack/draw.html`: `.stack`, `.stack-labelled`, `.stack-compact`, `.stack-svg`, `.stack-label`, `.stack-role`, `.stack-lead`, `.stack-dot`, `.stack-bracket`, `.stack-times`, `.stack-missing`, `.stack-cap`). A layer kit of 27 SVGs in `assets/icons/layers/`, line art with texture as strokes (seeds, stipple, hatching, grill char), redrawn from reference illustrations in Not a Chef's mockup; `patty-thin` and `patty-thick` are griddled, `patty-thin-grilled` and `patty-thick-grilled` have grill marks. On a build page the first block goes beside the picks (`.build-diagram`, now filled) instead of in the body, and a compact copy goes on the build's card (`partials/stack-strip.html`). An unknown layer or a bad `repeat:` warns.

- Methods and blueprints (`[params.fugu.methods]`: `folder`, `tag`, `taxonomy`, `label`, `icon`). A page in the essay section's `methods/` folder is a method: a method chip (`.method-chip`, fan-out icon, with a popover) replaces its folder name on its card and leads its page's tags row, and the methods tag is dropped from its plain tags. The methods tag's term page, when its `_index.md` sets `kind: method`, is the methods list (`partials/method/list.html`): the definition, each blueprint with its parts and builds (`.bp`, `.parts`, `.opt-pill`), then standalone methods as cards. A blueprint term page (`partials/blueprint/page.html`) shows build cards (`.build-card`, `.build-profile`), a table comparing their picks (`.builds-compared`), the parts in its `parts` order with each method's `## Options` as pills, recipes grouped by `build_name` (`.recipe-group`), and further reading. A build page (a method with `build_name`, `profile`, `picks`) shows its profile under the title, its picks before the body (`.build-picks`, `.picks`), and the recipes made from it after (`.build-recipes`). A part's method or a build ends with part navigation (`.part-nav`) in place of previous/next. The blueprint taxonomy gets a chip in the tags row (`.blueprint-chip`, `.blueprint-chip-part`) in place of Blowfish's badge. `recipes/index.json` gains `blueprint` and `build_name`.
- Build history: a build page's `origin` (free text) shows under its name on its card (`.build-origin`) and under its profile on its page (`.build-origin-lead`), and its `era` (a year) places it on a lineage timeline that opens the blueprint's page, before Builds (`partials/blueprint/lineage.html`: `.timeline`, `.tl-track`, `.tl-list`, `.tl-mark`, `.alt` for a label raised clear of a close neighbour, `.todo` for planned, `.tl-name`, `.tl-detail`, `.tl-dot`, `.tl-ticks`, `.tl-tick`; a list under 640px). A blueprint's `planned: ["year: name"]` adds faint dots for unwritten builds. Builds are now ordered by `era`, undated last, on the blueprint's page, the methods list, and in part navigation.
- Shared concept partials (`partials/concept/`): the definition callout, the chip popover, the kind icons, and the further-reading list, used by principle tags, drafts, and methods. The definition callout now carries its kind's class (`.k-principle`, `.k-method`) and icon beside its label, so a principle tag's page changes there. A tag's `kind: principle` works like `principle: true`.
- Every regular page links an RSS feed in its `<head>`: its section's, else its first section's, else the site's (pages have none of their own), so a reader given a recipe link can find the recipes feed.
- Basic styles: `assets/css/fugu.css` styles every class Fugu emits (recipe page, ingredients and the scale/units panel, sidebars, cards, list pages, formula diagrams, popovers, drafts, photos), coloured from the site's Blowfish scheme through `--fugu-*` custom properties, in light and dark. `head.html` bundles it after Blowfish's CSS and before the site's `custom.css`. `params.fugu.styles = false` leaves it out.
- Docs, in `docs/`: `getting-started.md` (an empty site to a working recipe page), `recipe-format.md` (writing a recipe), and `frontmatter.md` (every field Fugu reads).
- `docs/configuration.md`: every `[params.fugu]` setting and the Blowfish settings Fugu depends on.
- `docs/features.md`: a tour of what Fugu adds, as a reader sees it.
- Drafts as pages that are published but still changing, for a site that builds drafts. `[params.fugu.development]` sets the words (`label`, `short` for the card chip, `message`, `note`, `more`, `heading`, `empty`), an optional icon (`icon`, an SVG in the site's assets, inlined; a dot otherwise), and the page that explains drafts (`page`). A draft gets a banner under its title and tags, at the end of the page header (`partials/development/banner.html`, `.dev-strip`), and on its card a chip that leads the top row and takes a slot (`development/mark.html`, `.dev-chip`). A page's `working_on` frontmatter shows under the banner. The `page` lists every draft under `heading`, in place of its related content (`development/list.html`, in `.related-section.development-list`). The defaults say "In development".
- `working_on` in `recipes/index.json`.
- A cuisine's term page (`content/cuisine/<name>/_index.md`) can set `short`, shown on recipe cards in place of the full name (which becomes its title), like a principle tag's `short`.
- The recipe archetype lists every optional frontmatter field (empty), and starts a new recipe as a draft with an empty `working_on`.

- Personal notes: `params.fugu.hideIntroQuotes` (default `true`) hides blockquotes in a recipe's intro, and `params.fugu.headings.hidden` (default `["History"]`) hides whole sections; `false` and `[]` turn them off. This only hides the text from readers: it's still in the HTML, the search index, and the RSS feed.
- `[params.fugu.headings]`: the `##` titles that mean `method`, `mechanic`, `to-serve`, `variations`, `notes`, `equipment`, and `hidden` (lists of whole titles, any case), plus `notIngredients`, the words that keep a recipe heading from being read as an ingredient section. The defaults are the old fixed names, so a site writing in English with Fugu's section names sets nothing.
- Principle tags work for any tag whose `content/tags/<slug>/_index.md` sets `principle: true`, not just `win-the-fridge`. Its essay is the page named by `essay` there, else the reference essay carrying the tag; its chip on recipe cards reads `short`, else the tag's title. Sites that relied on the card's old "WTF" label set `short: WTF`.
- `params.fugu.referenceEssays` (default `reference-essays`, a folder in the essay section) and `params.fugu.glossaryPage` (default `glossary`, a page in the reference section): where `[[wiki links]]` find glossary terms. `""` turns either off.
- `params.fugu.recipeSidebar`: the recipe sidebar's section boxes, in order (`photos`, `to-serve`, `mechanic`, `variations`, `equipment`, `notes`). A key left out keeps that section in the article; an unknown key warns. The default is the old fixed order, so a site that doesn't set it renders the same.
- Formula strip on recipe cards: a page with a ```` ```formula ```` block shows its icons and operators (and a ratio's numbers) as a small row under the card's intro, with each ingredient's label, quantity, and swaps in a hover tooltip (`partials/formula-strip.html`). Cards with a strip get `.has-formula`; the site styles `.rcard-formula`, `.rcard-formula-link` and `.slot`.
- `tools/compare-builds.py`, later: fingerprinted bundle names (`main.bundle.min.<hash>.css`) and integrity hashes are ignored when comparing, and the renamed bundle is diffed as one file, so a CSS change shows up once instead of on every page.
- `tools/compare-builds.py`: builds a site with Fugu at a git ref and with the working tree, and lists every file that differs (whitespace ignored), so a theme change can't alter a site by accident.
- Fixture sites in `tests/sites/` (`recipes-only` and `everything`) and a GitHub Actions job that builds both with `--panicOnWarning` against Hugo 0.161.1 and Blowfish `e9699d8`.
- `tests/check.py`: builds every fixture site and checks its pages against its `expect.txt`; `--compare` also runs `compare-builds.py`. CI runs it. A third fixture, `renamed`, moves every section to a new name and is expected to fail until the templates read section names from config.
- `--help` for every tool, including `add-image.sh`.
- A scope statement in the README: only recipes are required.

### Changed

The sidebar has search, part and build counts, "On this page", and the pages linked under `## Further reading`, labelled by kind, which no longer sit at the foot of the body (`.blueprint-further` is gone).

Content is in the body now, so these are gone: `[params.fugu.methods]` `taxonomy`; the frontmatter fields `blueprint`, `part`, `build_name`, `profile`, `origin`, `picks`, and a blueprint's `parts` and `planned`; the builds comparison table (`.builds-compared`); recipes grouped by build and by part on the blueprint page (`.recipe-group`, `.part-recipes`); a build page's profile, origin, picks, and recipes (`method/build-head.html`, `build-picks.html`, `build-recipes.html`, `picks.html`, `place.html`; `.build-picks`, `.picks`, `.build-recipes`, `.build-profile`); the methods list's "Compare picks" link and its "to come" parts; and `blueprint` and `build_name` in `recipes/index.json`. The stack render hook draws every block where it's written. The fixtures' blueprints are folders.
- A principle tag's page (`_default/term.html`) is on the reading layout, like the methods list (`.principle-layout`, `.principle-page`). The header is the title and its "principle" kind, without the count; the definition is a plain intro (`.principle-intro`, ending in the `.read-essay` link) in place of the callout; the body's search box is gone. The sidebar has search, counts (recipes, essays) and the chip as cards show it ("On cards"), and further reading, the full essay first, moved out of the body. Plain tag and cuisine pages are unchanged.

- The methods list (`method/list.html`, the methods section's page and the methods tag's) is on the reading layout: a body column and the reading sidebar's wrap (`.method-list-layout`). The header is the title alone; the definition is a plain intro (`.method-intro`) in place of the callout; the sidebar has search, counts in the date-rows style (methods, blueprints, builds, parts written), "On this page" (`#blueprints` with a `#bp-<slug>` per blueprint, `#techniques`; counts as `.rs-toc-top .n`), and further reading, moved out of the body. A blueprint's builds sit under its block as a rail: ‹ › buttons (`.bp-rail-prev`, `.bp-rail-next`) and dots (`.bp-rail-dots`), run by `assets/js/builds-rail.js`, which sets `.is-overflowing` and `.at-end` on `.bp-builds`. ### Fixed

- The link render hook emits only its element, without the template's whitespace around it, so punctuation right after a link ("(see [[Seasoning burgers]]).") no longer wraps onto its own line or reads as a separate word. Word counts, reading times, and auto summaries change slightly to match.
- A build card's stack diagram is centred between its intro and its stats when a taller card in the row stretches it, rather than sitting under the text with a gap below.
- A method's reading sidebar labels its linked recipes "Recipes" rather than "Recipes in this essay", since its sidebar already says "On this page". A blueprint part's or build's sidebar lists the other parts in weight order, or builds in era order, as the blueprint page does, rather than alphabetically.
- A page's meta description no longer renders fenced code blocks in its intro, so a formula or stack block before the first heading isn't drawn (and warned about) a second time or read into the description.
- A draft recipe card with both a cuisine and a principle tag no longer fails the build (`recipe-card.html` ran out of tag slots and called `first` with a negative count).
- `cuisine` can be a list (`cuisine: [Thai]`) as well as a string; it used to fail the build on recipe cards. The card shows the first and skips tags repeating any of them.
- Swiping between photos in the photo viewer works on iOS. Safari took a sideways swipe as scrolling and cancelled it, and a swipe that ended off the photo closed the viewer.

## 2026-10-06: split from Not a Chef

Templates, render hooks, JS, formula icons, archetypes, and the content tools moved out of [Not a Chef](https://github.com/robotpony/not-a-chef) into this repo, unchanged. Not a Chef uses it as a git submodule at `themes/fugu`.
